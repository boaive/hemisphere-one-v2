# Mixamo clips: the short way

1. In Chrome, open https://www.mixamo.com and click **Log in** (top right). Sign in with an Adobe ID (free).
2. Click each link below. The right animation is the **first card**: click it.
3. Click the orange **DOWNLOAD** button on the right, then **DOWNLOAD** again in the box that opens. Do not change anything.
4. Repeat for all eight. Then reply "done": I collect the files from your Downloads folder.
